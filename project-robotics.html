Project 02

Robotics

My journey in robotics including FLL (First Lego League) competitions and hands-on engineering projects. Building robots and solving real-world challenges.

First Lego League

Participating in First Lego League has been an incredible experience. Working with a team to design, build, and program robots to complete specific challenges has taught me invaluable skills in engineering, problem-solving, and teamwork.

Robot Design Process

The robot design process involves brainstorming ideas, creating prototypes, testing, and iterating. Each season brings new challenges that require creative solutions and innovative thinking.

Programming

Programming the robots involves using block-based and text-based coding to create autonomous behaviors. Debugging and optimizing code is a crucial part of making the robot perform consistently during competitions.

Competition Experience

Competitions are the highlight of the robotics experience. The energy, collaboration, and sportsmanship create an environment where learning and fun go hand in hand. Each competition teaches something new.

Skills Learned

Through robotics, I have developed skills in mechanical design, programming, teamwork, time management, and public speaking. These experiences have shaped my interest in STEM and inspired me to pursue engineering further.
