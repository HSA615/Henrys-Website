Project 01

ArtifactStory.com

A website my teammates and I built for an FLL competition. It was designed to tell our project story clearly, show our research, and make the experience easy to explore.

Where It All Started

It was the begining of a First Lego League (FLL) competition and a group of students named the Mechanical Maniacs where brainstorming an inovational idea that would help Archeologist. I was the team captain for the group. We decided to make a website where archologist can document their finding imagine a Wayback machine for archeology. We wanted the website to be accesible to everyone and include Text To Speach (TTS) and support for multiple languages.

The Process

The whole process involved several stages of planning, design, and development, but I'll summarize it to the key points. We first started by having AI generate us the "skeleton" of the website. We will change it later but first we needed somewhere to start. Without AI it would have taken forever to make, each webpage alone was already hundreds of lines of code. Once we had the "skeleton" made we customized it by adding our logo and pictures of real artifacts. We then made a script to present it to the judges during FFL Qualifier.
